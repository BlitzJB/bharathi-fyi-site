# bharathi.fyi

register: brand

## Product purpose

Personal site of Joshua Bharathi, an AI engineer with a platform
engineering background. Three surfaces: a landing page with a live
knowledgebase-grounded AI chat, an MDX blog, and a public operations page
(/ops, "the engine room") exposing the deliberately overengineered serving
infrastructure behind the chat. The site itself is the portfolio piece: the
overengineering is the exhibit, and /ops is the glass that makes it
visible.

## Users

Recruiters, senior engineers, and potential collaborators evaluating
Bharathi. They arrive skeptical of AI-generated portfolio slop and are
convinced by evidence: real latency numbers, real traces, real admission
control. The /ops page is read by engineers who run production systems and
will notice fake-looking dashboards instantly.

## Brand and tone

Minimalist editorial. Confident, precise, understated; numbers over
adjectives. Copy reads like a person wrote it: short sentences, no em
dashes, no "not X but Y" constructions, no AI vocabulary (landscape,
leverage, journey, robust, delve). Senior-level framing throughout, no
youth or stat-tile bragging.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS dashboards: gradient heroes, glassmorphism, stat-tile
  grids with big numbers and tiny labels.
- Dark "observability" clichés: neon greens on black, fake terminal chrome.
- Anything that reads "vibecoded" or template-bought.

## Strategic principles

- Show, don't claim: every capability on the site is demonstrated live.
- The infrastructure narrative (queue-fed serving, degradation ladders,
  eval gates) is the differentiator; design should make it legible.
- One page, one idea, generous whitespace, hairline structure.
