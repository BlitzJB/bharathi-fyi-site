Generate LinkedIn post ideas. Seed (topic, recent work, or blank): $ARGUMENTS

1. Load the voice profile for core topics and goal mix. If the seed is blank, also ask the 2-Year Test questions (Ship 30): "What can you do today that you couldn't 2 years ago?" / "What were you struggling with then that's easy now?" — and Bourgoin's trigger lens: "What moment is your reader in when they need this?"
2. Build a matrix (Welsh): the user's themes × styles — Observational, Actionable, Motivational, Analytical, Contrarian, X vs Y, Present vs Future, Listicle. Cross-check each idea against Ship 30's 4A angles (Actionable / Analytical / Aspirational / Anthropological) and niche the topic down two levels ("investing" → "investing in your 20s to buy property in your 30s").
3. Produce 10–15 ideas. Each: working hook line, one-sentence angle, credibility stance (expert / curator / personal experience), engagement goal, suggested format (carousel/image/text). Prioritize ideas that hit the Golden Intersection: personal story + practical takeaway.
4. Kill any idea that would fail the substance gate (no metric, no insight, no mechanism) unless an interview could rescue it — mark those "needs interview".
5. Append the keepers to `research/idea-bank.md` (create if missing) and show the top 5 with your recommendation.
