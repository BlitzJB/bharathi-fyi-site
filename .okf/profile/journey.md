# The arc

## Chapter 1 — Shipping for real clients (2022–2024)

Bharathi started early: while beginning his B.Tech, he founded **Blitz Design
and Development** (Nov 2022 – Mar 2024), building traffic-bearing e-commerce
backends and internal software — billing, HR utilities, CRMs, fleet
management, automations — for **20+ clients across India**. This is where the
platform instinct formed: other people's businesses ran on his uptime.

## Chapter 2 — Backend and platform engineering (2024–2025)

Internships at **Dexio Designs** and **Miniture** (services, notification
systems, data ingest, a React Native app he owned a significant portion of),
then the most platform-heavy chapter: **MotorQ** (SDE Intern, mid-2025),
where he benchmarked Apache Pulsar, designed an **Event Queue Abstraction
layer** making the backend agnostic across Event Hubs/Kafka/Pulsar, led a
**zero-downtime migration** of a customer-facing API onto it, and rebuilt
multi-tenant monitoring on VictoriaMetrics with a GitOps-driven rule-generation
pipeline.[^resume-2025]

## Chapter 3 — AI engineering (now)

His degree is in AI & Data Science, and AI has run through his project work
from the start (CourseGPT's LLM generation pipeline, KHackSim's simulation
modeling). The current chapter turns that into the day job: building
LLM-backed products and the platform discipline they're missing.

# How to talk about this

The honest framing is *continuity, not reinvention*: the same skills that
made event pipelines reliable — observability, failure-mode thinking,
zero-downtime change — are exactly what production AI systems currently lack.
Bharathi is early-career with an outsized shipping record: real clients at
18, national hackathon wins, and platform work trusted with customer-facing
traffic.
